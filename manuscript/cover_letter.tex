\opening{Dear Prof. Zhang and Editorial Board,}

I am pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled:
\begin{center}
\textbf{``CoDe-CBF: Distribution-Free Safety Filtering for Decentralized Multi-UAV Swarms under Non-Stationary Aerodynamic Disturbances and Variable Communication Age-of-Information''}
\end{center}
for consideration as a regular research letter in the \textit{IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)}.

\textbf{Motivation and Significance:}
Decentralized multi-UAV swarms operating in confined corridors encounter two simultaneous, coupled failure modes: unmodeled aerodynamic rotor downwash (inducing severe downward accelerations exceeding $-4.2\text{ m/s}^2$) and stochastic wireless communication packet loss over ROS~2 DDS middleware, resulting in stale neighbor state estimates with variable Age-of-Information (AoI). Existing High-Order Control Barrier Functions (HOCBFs) fail under these conditions: nominal methods cause frequent boundary breaches, worst-case robust CBFs cause severe deadlock freezes, and offline conformal approaches break down under flight distribution shifts.

\textbf{Core Scientific Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Tilt-Aware Downwash Tensor:} We derive an anisotropic metric tensor $E_j(v_j)$ that dynamically rotates the barrier ellipsoid along the physical rotor slipstream as a function of forward flight speed, eliminating dangerous under-approximations in trailing wakes.
    \item \textbf{Cyber-Physical Nonconformity Decomposition:} We formulate an Age-of-Information normalized nonconformity score and establish fine-grained dual-rate attribution separating physical atmospheric wind residuals from communication latency staleness.
    \item \textbf{Finite-Horizon Regret Guarantees:} We rigorously prove that under arbitrary non-stationary wind shear and stochastic packet drops, the empirical safety violation rate converges to $\alpha_{\mathrm{target}}$ with sub-linear cumulative regret $\mathcal{R}_T \le \mathcal{O}(1/T)$ (Theorem~3), asymptotic coverage (Theorem~1), strictly bounded parameter dynamics (Lemma~1), and recursive feasibility (Theorem~2).
    \item \textbf{100\% Real-World Data Grounding:} The framework is validated across a 1,000-seed Monte Carlo sweep and live ROS~2 Humble flight nodes driven by 2,221 real 3D sonic anemometer wind records (NREL/AIJ), empirical quadrotor downwash datasets, and live ROS~2 DDS traces.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Superiority:} CoDe-CBF achieves an aggregate safety violation rate of only $0.60\%$ (and $3.01\%$ in narrow canyon bottlenecks), strictly satisfying the $5.0\%$ risk budget where nominal HOCBF fails ($11.35\%$), while solving in $<0.65\text{ ms}$ on edge CPU hardware.
\end{enumerate}

This manuscript represents original, unpublished work and is not concurrently submitted elsewhere. All authors have approved this submission. Complete source code, real-world datasets, and reproduction scripts have been prepared for open-source dissemination.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration of our work.
